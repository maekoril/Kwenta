# Kwenta approved seven-day story

**Status:** Approved and implemented 2026-10-23
**Narrative data:** `data/story/kwenta_7_day_story.json`

The game follows 12-year-old Tonyo for seven days while his mother, Aling Cora, is in the province caring for his sick Lola. Tonyo begins by avoiding school and responsibility. Running the sari-sari store teaches him to work carefully, account for money and utang, accept help, and value the people behind every transaction.

The campaign contains the prologue “Ang Susi,” Days 1–7 (“Ayoko Nito,” “Mali ang Sukli,” “Tara Na!,” “Ulan,” “Utang,” “Fiesta,” and “Kwenta”), six key choice flags, the smaller haggling callback, Aling Cora's conditional notebook reactions, the tumbang preso epilogue, and Tonyo's classroom presentation.

Implementation boundaries:

- Keep the established seller-side storefront, pixel-art direction, product art, drag-and-drop loop, and parody brands.
- Use short dialogue panels and existing portraits as simple character poses.
- Keep suitable events playable through the normal store shift; present haggling, utang, rain, rival-store, brownout, and other story-specific beats through dialogue choices where no dedicated mechanic exists.
- Remember choices without scoring them as good or bad.
- Allow the full seven-day story to restart after the ending.

This document and the narrative JSON supersede the older ten-shift summer/shelf-replacement story sections in historical project plans. Those older documents remain as implementation history rather than current narrative authority.
